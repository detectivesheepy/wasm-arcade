About Street Fighter II Deluxe

Street Fighter II' - Champion Edition is a 1992 Capcom arcade fighting game released as an export version and developed in Japan. It runs on Capcom Play System hardware, with the B-Board numbered 91635B-2. This export release is tied to the original Japanese version, Street Fighter II' - Champion Edition [B-Board 91634B-2], for full details about the game itself.
