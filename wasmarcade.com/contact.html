Copyright and takedown requests

If you own the rights to a game or other material on WASM Arcade and want it removed, email [email protected] with the subject line "Takedown request". Please include the page address (for example wasmarcade.com/flash-games/…), the work you own, and how we can reach you. The full list of what a notice needs, and what happens after you send it, is on our copyright and takedown policy page.

Reporting a bug

The more of this you include, the faster we can reproduce and fix it:

  • The game or page address.
  • Your browser and its version, and your device (for example "Chrome 129 on Windows 11, GTX 1660").
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • Any error shown on the screen. A screenshot helps.

Many loading problems are fixed by updating the browser, closing other heavy tabs, or turning off extensions that block scripts for the site.

What to expect

  • Takedown notices are reviewed first, and valid ones are acted on promptly.
  • Other messages are answered in order. We cannot reply to every suggestion, but we read them all.
  • We never ask for passwords or payment details by email.
