Minecraft in Your Browser: Java 1.8.8 on WebAssembly

Mine, build and survive in endless block worlds. Minecraft 1.8.8 running as a WebAssembly build, with your worlds saved on your own device.

Every world starts the same way: a blank horizon, a handful of trees and a night coming. Punch wood, craft a pickaxe, dig for coal and iron, and turn a hillside into a home before the zombies arrive. Survival mode gives you hunger, health and monsters; creative mode gives you unlimited blocks and the freedom to fly.

This page runs Minecraft Java Edition 1.8.8 through EaglercraftX, a community port that compiles the game to WebAssembly. It renders with WebGL 2.0 in a normal browser tab, with the classic 1.8 features intact: the Nether and the End, redstone, enchanting, brewing and the full mob list. Your worlds are stored in your browser, on your device. WASM Arcade is not affiliated with Mojang Studios or Microsoft.

Frequently asked questions

Which version of Minecraft is this?

Java Edition 1.8.8, the "Combat Update" era release, running through EaglercraftX, a community port to WebAssembly. Later features such as bees, the Nether Update and the new world height are not in 1.8.

Where are my worlds saved?

In your browser storage, on your own device. Nothing is uploaded to a server. Clearing site data for wasmarcade.com deletes them, so use the in-game world export to keep a backup.

Can I play multiplayer?

This page is set up for singleplayer. The port includes multiplayer and LAN options that depend on third-party relay servers, which WASM Arcade does not run or vouch for.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

It is built for a keyboard and mouse, and there are no on-screen touch controls, so a desktop or laptop is strongly recommended.

How do I get my mouse back?

Press Esc. That opens the pause menu and releases the mouse. In fullscreen, hold Esc a little longer to leave fullscreen; the browser requires that extra moment.
