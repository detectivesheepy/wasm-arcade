Privacy policy

This policy explains what information is handled when you use wasmarcade.com ("WASM Arcade", "we", "us"), who handles it, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • You do not need an account, and we never ask for your name, email or password to play.
  • Your game saves and settings stay in your own browser. We cannot see them.
  • The site is paid for by advertising. Our advertising partners, including Google, may use cookies to show and measure ads. You can turn off personalized ads at any time (see your choices).
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not build profiles of our visitors.

What we collect

WASM Arcade has no accounts, no sign-up forms and no comments, so we do not collect personal information directly. Like every website, our servers and our network provider receive the technical details every browser sends with a request: your IP address, browser and operating system, the page requested, the referring page and the time. We use these only to deliver the site, keep it secure and fix problems, and our server logs are deleted automatically after seven days.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply and to handle your request, and we do not add you to any mailing list.

Storage on your device

The games need to remember things between visits. They do this in your browser, not on our servers:

  • Game saves are stored in your browser's IndexedDB storage. The Save Manager on each game can export them to a file, import them, or delete them.
  • Settings such as volume, controls and graphics options are kept in local storage.
  • Game files may be cached by your browser so they do not download again on every visit.
  • Sponsor preferences: local and session storage also hold which advertising partner your browser is assigned for sponsor pages and when one last opened, so we do not open them too often.

This information never leaves your device. Clearing your browser's site data removes it, including your saves, so export any save you want to keep first.

Advertising and cookies

WASM Arcade is paid for by advertising. We work with these advertising partners:

  • Google AdSense, which may display ads on our pages (see the next section).
  • Monetag, whose sponsor pages open in a new browser tab when you press Play on a game, and, on our browsing pages (the home page and the lists of games, never on a game itself), may open an ad in a new tab behind the page after you click. These are ordinary tabs that you can close. Monetag runs a small script on those browsing pages to do this.

Advertising partners and the third-party vendors they work with may use cookies, web beacons, device identifiers and similar technologies to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure results and prevent fraud. Where allowed, they may use information about your visits to this and other websites to show ads that are more relevant to you. They receive your IP address and browser details when they serve an ad, and they handle that information under their own privacy policies: Google and Monetag.

WASM Arcade itself does not set advertising cookies and has no access to the cookies our partners set.

Google AdSense

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising from Google in Google Ads Settings, and learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • Other third-party vendors and ad networks may also serve ads on this site. You can opt out of many of them at aboutads.info (US) and youronlinechoices.eu (Europe).

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before Google uses cookies for personalized ads, through a consent message certified under the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. You can change your choice at any time from that message.

Your choices and opt-outs

  • Personalized ads: turn them off in Google Ads Settings or at aboutads.info. You will still see ads, but they will be less tailored.
  • Cookies: every major browser lets you block or delete cookies, including third-party cookies. Blocking them does not stop the games from working.
  • Sponsor tabs: your browser's pop-up blocker can stop them; the game starts either way.

Analytics and hosting

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It does not use cookies and does not identify individual visitors. The site is delivered through Cloudflare's network, which processes connection details to route traffic and protect the site from attacks, under Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Links to other websites, including sponsor pages, are not covered by this policy. Please read the privacy policy of any site you visit.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK GDPR and US state privacy laws (including California's CCPA/CPRA) give you rights over your personal information: to know what is held about you, to get a copy, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict its use, and to opt out of its sale or sharing for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information for money.

Because we hold almost nothing about you, most requests are best directed to the advertising partner concerned, using the links above. For anything we hold, such as an email you sent us, write to [email protected] and we will respond within the time the law requires. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights. Where our partners process data for advertising, the legal basis is your consent (in the EEA and UK) or our legitimate interest in funding a free service.

Children

WASM Arcade is a general-audience site and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA and UK). Some games in the collection were originally rated for older players. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy when our partners or practices change, and the date at the top of the page will change with it. For any question about privacy, email [email protected] or visit the contact page.
