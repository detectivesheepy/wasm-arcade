About Shop Empire Fable

In Shop Empire Fable, the player takes charge of shops in a fantasy kingdom and tries to make them profitable. A bookstore and a clothing boutique are among the businesses that can be opened. Success depends on building a retail empire across the realm, with pixel art visuals setting the scene. The game was developed by Little Giant World.
