What WASM Arcade is

WASM Arcade is an advertising-supported library of game engines compiled to WebAssembly, played in a web browser. The flagship games run on open-source engine re-implementations and ports, and the Flash games run in Ruffle, an open-source Flash Player emulator. Everything runs on your own device; we do not stream games from a server.

We may add, change or remove games and features at any time, including in response to a request from a rights holder.
