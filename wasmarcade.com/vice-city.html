Grand Theft Auto: Vice City in Your Browser

Experience the complete 1986 neon Miami adventure in high definition directly in your browser. Powered by WebAssembly and WebGL2 with zero downloads.

Released into the sun-soaked criminal underworld of 1986 Vice City after a fifteen-year prison term, Tommy Vercetti is sent south by the Forelli syndicate to oversee an ambitious narcotics expansion. When a violent ambush strips him of both the cash and the product, Tommy refuses to retreat. Instead, he sets out to conquer the city from the ground up—forging uneasy alliances, taking down rival cartels, establishing lucrative front businesses, and seizing total control of the metropolis.

WASM Arcade brings this landmark 3D open world straight to your modern web browser using bleeding-edge WebAssembly (WASM) and hardware-accelerated WebGL 2.0. There are no installers, no plugin requirements, and no compromise on fidelity: enjoy the complete original campaign, all nine legendary radio stations, dynamic weather, responsive mouse aim, full gamepad support, and persistent browser safehouse saves.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install or download anything to play GTA Vice City?

No installation or software download is required. The entire game streams securely into your browser sandbox via WebAssembly and WebGL2. Once loaded, assets are saved locally in your browser cache for near-instant repeat launches.

How do saved games work? Will my story progress be kept?

Every time you save at the Ocean View Hotel or Vercetti Estate, your save game is written to your browser’s persistent IndexedDB storage. Your progress remains safe between sessions. You can also use our Save Game Manager to download backup .b save files or import a completed 100% save at any time.

Can I use an Xbox, PlayStation, or Bluetooth controller?

Yes. WASM Arcade features native HTML5 Gamepad API integration. Simply connect your Xbox, PlayStation DualSense/DualShock, or generic PC Bluetooth controller to your device, and the buttons and analog sticks will automatically map to Vice City controls.

How does WASM Arcade compare to older browser game ports?

WASM Arcade was engineered as a high-performance web gaming experience. While older browser ports often lock gameplay to 30 FPS and subject players to intrusive redirects and laggy audio, WASM Arcade delivers uncapped 60+ FPS high-refresh gaming, discrete GPU auto-acceleration, instant save file import/export, and a clean, responsive gaming experience.

Can I play GTA Vice City on a Chromebook, MacBook, or mobile device?

Yes. Because the game executes entirely inside modern browsers with WebGL2 support, it runs smoothly on Chromebooks, macOS, Linux, Windows, and modern tablets. On touchscreens, intuitive on-screen virtual gamepads and analog sticks activate automatically.

How do I activate cheat codes while playing?

Just like the original PC release, simply type the cheat code directly on your keyboard at any moment during gameplay (e.g., type ASPIRINE for full health or PANZER for a tank). You can also click on our in-game Cheats menu button to inject any code with a single click.
